# KAMARI Goa: asset inventory, permissions and unverified information

Written for: the project owner and anyone who will decide whether this concept goes public.

## Source material

The project folder held 102 files: 54 JPEG images and 48 MP4 videos, all exported from the Instagram account @kamarigoa (filenames begin `kamarigoa_<date>_…`). The folder contained no logo file, no Instagram PDF, no font files, no official menu, and no event listings. The originals were not modified; web derivatives were written to `site/public/media/`.

Every still and poster was reviewed on contact sheets. Each video was checked across several timepoints, not just its first frame. The first-frame check missed a DJ and diners in the mirror-arch clip, which is why the review was extended.

## Excluded, and why

- **Promotional graphics with overlaid copy** (for example "Save this for your next Goa weekend", "Your next Goa stop", "Good food, great drinks", "Capture the sunset", "Hear the moments", "The last weekend"). They are KAMARI's own marketing and carry text that would clash with the site. They also carry the logo and copy the owner has not approved for reuse.
- **Clips with identifiable people**: the DJ and diners in 2026-10-02 (the first hero candidate), 2026-08-21 (bartenders), 2026-09-05 (overlays and guests), 2026-10-29 series (guests toasting), 2026-09-30 series (overlays and a performer). Public use needs written consent from each person.
- **Clips with on-screen labels or overlays**: 2026-10-05 #2 (label), 2026-06-21 #3 (print copy), 2026-07-17 series (recipe text), 2026-06-13 (overlay), 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-24 sets (promo copy).
- **Footage of other businesses**: the 2026-08-22 aerial and the 2026-10-02 aerial show neighbouring venues. Not usable as KAMARI footage.
- **Performer and event imagery**: costumed performers, party and DJ scenes (2026-10-06 set and others). Performer identities and event participation are unconfirmed, so nothing is captioned as an event.
- **Audio**: all 48 videos carry third-party or original audio. Audio is stripped from every derived file. No music is used anywhere on the site.

## Publication permissions still needed

Every published asset is pending authorization, including the ones with no people. They belong to KAMARI's Instagram. Before any public launch, get written permission from KAMARI for:

1. All 16 published media files (brand-owned photography and video).
2. The two published clips showing the sculpture terrace and gloved hands. Confirm the gloved hands belong to a staff member who is willing to be shown, or replace the clip.
3. The official logo, which was not supplied. The site uses a typographic wordmark until it is.
4. Any photographs that include guests, staff or performers, if they are added later.

## Unverified business information

Each item below is displayed as "to be confirmed" or not shown at all.

- **Full address.** Only "128/20, Dmello Vaddo, Goa" was supplied, and it is partial.
- **Opening hours.** Not supplied.
- **Menu, prices, dishes, ingredients, allergens, dietary classes.** Not supplied. The Savour page shows a showcase and asks visitors to call.
- **Drinks names, recipes, prices, alcohol content.** Not supplied. The Elixirs page names no drinks.
- **Events.** None verified, so the events list is empty and shows an empty state with the Instagram link and phone numbers.
- **Ownership, architectural history, design credits, cuisine classification, awards, ratings, reviews.** Not supplied, so none are claimed.
- **WhatsApp.** Neither number is verified for WhatsApp, so no WhatsApp link is offered.
- **Reservation destination.** None authorized. The enquiry form is a labelled demonstration and sends nothing.
- **Brand colours.** The palette in `src/styles/tokens.css` is the provisional palette from the brief. It has not been sampled from the official logo.
- **Typefaces.** Cormorant Garamond and Manrope (both SIL Open Font License) are used as stand-ins. The brand's own type has not been identified.
